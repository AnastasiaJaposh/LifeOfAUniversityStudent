In this small game, the player lives through a university student for a day.

Once you enter a specific setting, first you have to read the story. 
After the story is finished, you have to find the "interactable object", 
which gives you a choice between 2-3 things. Depending on your choice, 
points will be accumulated and your next setting will be determined. 
After the playthrough is done, theres a screen which informs you about 
what happened the next day, as well as, a small analysis paragraph. 
After the ending screen, you can press ENTER to see your stats 
and after that the playthrough is finished.

controls:
walking around: arrow keys
navigating through text: ENTER
interacting: E
choosing a character: Mouse click

The assets and the specific parts of the backgrounds are taken from free 
tile-sets and then constructed by me.
The start window image is taken from pinterest.
