<h2>Description</h2>
<p>In this small game, the player lives through a university student for a day.<br>

Once you enter a specific setting, first you have to read the story. 
After the story is finished, you have to find the "interactable object", 
which gives you a choice between 2-3 things. Depending on your choice, 
points will be accumulated and your next setting will be determined. 
After the playthrough is done, there will be a screen which informs you about 
what happened the next day, as well as, a small paragraph analyzing the choices made. 
After the ending screen, you can press ENTER to see your stats 
and after that the playthrough is finished.</p>

<h2>Conrols</h2>
walking around: arrow keys<br>
navigating through text: ENTER<br>
interacting: E<br>
choosing a character: Mouse click<br>
